# Privacy Policy

## 1. Zero-PII Standard
Conxian Gateway adheres to a "Zero-PII" standard. We do not store personally identifiable information. All cryptographic attestations are verified statelessy.

## 2. Data Transmission
Data transmitted through the gateway (such as ISO 20022 messages) is processed in-memory for verification and normalization. Settlement logs are linked to native blockchain transaction hashes, not user identities.

## 3. Institutional Governance
As an institutional middleware, privacy controls are enforced at the organization level. Audit logs and metrics are protected by Bearer token authentication.
